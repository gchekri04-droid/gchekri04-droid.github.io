AlarmLens ↗

  • Python
  • pandas
  • ISA-18.2
  • Ollama

An alarm-management analyzer for SCADA alarm logs. It reads a CSV export and measures the alarm system against ISA-18.2 / EEMUA 191 style benchmarks. The one-page report shows bad actors, chattering and stale alarms, flood episodes and the priority mix, with prioritized rationalization actions. An optional summary comes from an offline AI model, so no plant data leaves the PC.

8 alarm KPIsAlarms per day and per 10 min, time in flood, top-10 share, more
Finds bad actorsChattering, stale and flood-causing tags
Operator responseTime to acknowledge, and the alarms operators ignore
Drop and analyzeDrag in any SCADA CSV export; columns are auto-detected
