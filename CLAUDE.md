# Content rules for this repository (strict, always apply)

This is public. Before adding or changing any content, follow these rules without exception:

- Never include dollar amounts, prices paid, account balances, trade sizes or any money figures from personal finances.
- Never include account names, account numbers, broker or exchange account details, or which brokers/apps are used for personal money.
- Never include stock holdings, positions or specific trades.
- Never include savings plans, budgets, loans, salary or pay.
- Never include employer or client company names, job titles, dates of employment or years of experience. Describe skills, capabilities and achievements instead.
- Never include personal identifiers: email, phone, address, ID, visa or passport details.

Before every publish, scan the changed files for these items and remove anything that matches.
